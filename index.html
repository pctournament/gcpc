Tournament Timeline 🕰️

Week 1 — Qualification Week
Players participate in a qualification match hosted by a Match Clipper. The match is recorded and reviewed by the PC Executive Team, who determine each player's starting Pro Circuit rank.

Week 2 — MMR Week
Players compete in ranked matches to earn MMR and climb the leaderboard. Every match is an opportunity to improve your standing and move closer to the Top 10.

Week 3 — MMR Week
Continue playing ranked matches and earning MMR. At the end of the week, the Top 10 players on the leaderboard qualify for the playoffs.

Week 4 — Playoffs
The season concludes with a Single Elimination Bracket featuring the Top 10 MMR players. Winners advance through each round until a PC Champion is crowned, while the top three players earn a podium finish for the season.
